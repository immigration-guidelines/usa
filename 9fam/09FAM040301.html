9 FAm 403.1-1  OVERVIEW

(CT:VISA-1682;   01-04-2023)

The nonimmigrant visa (NIV) process is presented in detail in 9 FAM 403.2 through 9 FAM 403.10.  You must be sure that all staff follow the requirements for NIV processing, from receipt of application, proper collection of biometrics and required fees, interview by a consular officer, requisite namechecking and other clearances, to case adjudication, issuance or refusal, and, for issuances, consideration of reciprocity, annotations, and the printing and placement of the visa foil.  9 FAM 403.11 discusses visa revocation procedures, including when you may and may not revoke a visa.
