9 FAM 303.8-5(B)  (U) Background

(CT:VISA-2178;   09-08-2025)

a. (U) The Leahy Law prohibits assistance to foreign security force units and members of such units implicated in a gross violation of human rights (GVHR).  22 U.S.C. 2378d prohibits training, material or other forms of assistance to foreign security forces units or personnel if the Department has credible information that the unit or personnel has committed a GVHR, unless the Secretary determines that the government of the receiving state is taking effective steps to bring the responsible members of the security force to justice.
