9 FAM 402.7-2  OVERVIEW of K  and N Visas

(CT:VISA-910;   08-05-2019)

K and N visas are nonimmigrant visa classifications but are processed similarly to immigrant visas.  Information on these classifications is therefore found in the appropriate sections of 9 FAM 502.1 through 9 FAM 502.7.
