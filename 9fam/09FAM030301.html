9 FAM 303.1-2(D)(1)  (U) Fingerprinting

(CT:VISA-2027;   07-17-2024)

a. (U) Apart from the exemptions listed in 9 FAM 403.3-3, all NIV applicants ages 14 to 79 must be fingerprinted, and all IV applicants ages 14 and above must be fingerprinted.  In some limited cases, children aged 7 or over applying for an IV or NIV may be subject to fingerprinting. 

b. (U) The Automated Biometric Identification (IDENT) System works by checking visa applicant fingerprints against a database containing principally DHS and the Department's fingerprint data, with some other fingerprints from the FBI and DOD.  See 9 FAM 303.7 for information about IDENT and processing search results.  See 9 FAM 303.7 for information about the NGI.
