# Immigration guidance: the United States

Published guidance that the authorities of the United States issue to the officials who decide immigration cases, collected in November 2026.

- **Guidance:** 437 files in this folder. `index.tsv` lists each file with its title, date and source address.
- **Country-of-origin reports:** 771 files in `coi/`, with their own `coi/index.tsv`.
- The files are unmodified copies of what the source published. Where a source refused automated access, copies come from a public web archive; the index says which.
- What is missing, and how to obtain it, is described in [MISSING.md](https://github.com/immigration-guidelines/.github/blob/main/MISSING.md), in the repository that also holds the download scripts. Other jurisdictions are listed on the [organisation page](https://github.com/immigration-guidelines).

**Labour authority.** `labour/` holds the Department of Labor's Office of Foreign Labor Certification directives, Training and Employment Guidance Letters, prevailing-wage policy guidance, PERM bulletins and the 2021 H-1B bulletin on secondary employers, 43 documents from 1981 to 2026, all from Internet Archive copies because dol.gov refuses automated access.

The documents remain the work of the authorities that published them.
